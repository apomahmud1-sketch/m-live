## 1. Who we are

M Live is a live video social app for adults (18+) only. Only approved
hosts can go live. Viewers can watch, chat, send gifts, and apply to
become a host.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account information:** your name, phone number or email address
  (whichever you use to log in), and your profile information.
- **Camera and microphone:** only when you go live or join a live room
  with video/audio on. We do not record your camera or microphone
  outside a live session you joined.
- **Live content and messages:** video, audio and chat messages you
  send in live rooms and party rooms, so other people in the room can
  see and hear them.
- **Coins, gifts and wallet:** your coin balance, diamonds, earnings,
  gift history, recharge history and withdrawal requests (including the
  payment method and account details you give us for a withdrawal —
  bKash / Nagad / Bank / USDT).
- **Device information:** device type, app version and basic technical
  logs needed to keep the app working and to stop abuse.

## 3. How we use it

- To run the app: login, live video, chat, gifts, wallet and withdrawals.
- To approve hosts, review withdrawal requests, and keep the app safe.
- To reply to you if you contact support.
- We do **not** sell your personal information to anyone.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Other users:** what you show in a live room or in chat is visible
  to the people in that room — that is the point of a live app.
- **Service providers:** our video provider (Agora) carries the live
  video/audio, and our database provider (Supabase) stores your
  account and wallet data. They only process data to run the service.
- **Payments:** recharge and withdrawal payments are handled by the
  payment method you choose. We do not store your full bank/card
  details in the app.
- **Law:** we will share information if the law requires it.

## 5. Your choices

- You can edit your profile, delete messages you sent, and stop going
  live at any time.
- You can ask us to delete your account and your personal data by
  emailing the support address above. We will keep only what the law
  says we must keep (for example, payment records).
- You can turn off camera/microphone permission in your phone
  settings — but then you cannot go live.

## 6. Children

M Live is **18+ only**. If you are under 18, do not use this app.
If we learn that a user is under 18, we will close that account.

## 7. Data safety

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no internet service
is 100% secure. Do not share passwords, OTP codes or bank PINs with
anyone — including people who say they work for M Live.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in
the app. If you keep using M Live after a change, you accept the
new policy.
