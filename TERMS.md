  withdrawal is approved and paid (see section 5).
- Recharge payments are final, except where the law says otherwise.
  If a recharge fails, tell support with the payment proof.

## 5. Withdrawals — read this carefully

- We may delay or refuse a withdrawal if we suspect fraud, fake
  activity, or a broken rule.
- Real withdrawals need a real payment setup. Until that is set up,
  withdrawal requests in the app are recorded only — no real money
